# 12 · Terminals and data houses

This skill does not call another skill for data, and it does not ship a terminal login. Figures come from public pages through WebSearch and WebFetch. A host that does not answer is marked unavailable, with a substitute.

## 7.1 Terminals

| Terminal | What it is good for | Outside its home market |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bloomberg | Global macro, overseas companies, commodities, FX | The default global terminal |
| FactSet | Overseas financials and consensus | Public substitute: the filing plus a second broker note |
| Capital IQ | Comparables and deals | Public substitute: filings and exchange announcements |
| Wind | A-share, Hong Kong, and US-listed China names, plus notes and filings | Optional. A mainland terminal. If it does not answer, use EDGAR, HKEX, MOPS, and the company IR page |

## 7.2 Industry data houses

| House | Coverage |
| --- | --- |
| IDC / Gartner | Global IT, phones, PCs, servers, cloud |
| Canalys / Counterpoint | Phone, TWS, and smart-hardware units and share |
| TrendForce | Semiconductors, panels, memory, LED. Strong on the Taiwan chain |
| Omdia (formerly IHS) | Panels, displays, TVs, communications equipment |
| SEMI | Semiconductor equipment, materials, fabs |
| Yole Développement | Advanced packaging, MEMS, sensors, compound semiconductors |
| CAICT | Mainland China monthly phone units and policy. Optional: if it does not answer, use IDC, Canalys, or Counterpoint |

## Named in the tracking chapter, and easy to forget

| Source | Use | Frequency | If it does not answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DigiTimes | Chain news, especially the Taiwan supply chain | Daily | Say the item is unavailable. Do not invent a headline |
| Jiwei | Mainland chain news | Daily | Optional. Use DigiTimes |
| WitsView | Panel price | Weekly or monthly | Omdia |
| Sigmaintell | Monthly panel price | Monthly | Optional mainland house. Use WitsView or Omdia |
| DRAMeXchange | NAND / DRAM spot | Weekly | A second memory-price series, named |
| TSIA | Taiwan semiconductor industry statistics | Monthly | The company's own monthly revenue filing |
| SOX | Global semiconductor beta | Daily | The index publisher or Bloomberg |

Filing hosts, in order: the company's IR page, SEC EDGAR for a US name, HKEX for a Hong Kong name, MOPS for a Taiwan name. cninfo is the mainland A-share archive and is optional; it may not answer from outside mainland China.
