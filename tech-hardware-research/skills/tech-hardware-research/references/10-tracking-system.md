# 10 · The tracking sheet

Research is not a one-time note. It is a sheet you keep, and a judgment you revise.

## 5.1 Frequency

A high-frequency sheet is how a change shows up before the market writes it down.

| Frequency | What | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | SOX, Taiwan electronics, material filings, chain news | Bloomberg. DigiTimes. Company IR, EDGAR, HKEX, MOPS. Wind and Jiwei only if those hosts answer |
| Weekly | Panel price, NAND/DRAM spot, weekly phone units, channel inventory | WitsView, DRAMeXchange. Counterpoint or Canalys for phones. CAICT only if it answers |
| Monthly | Global phone, PC, and server units. Taiwan electronics monthly revenue | IDC, Gartner, SEMI, company filings, TSIA |
| Quarterly | Results, cloud capex, a fresh chain check, industry series | Filings and earnings calls |

`[extension]` Tracking is a reconciliation. Each row answers: versus the last assumption, is this above, in line, or below, and does the model change? A sheet that only records has no use. The template is `../assets/tracking-calendar.md`.

## 5.2 Three layers of sources

| Layer | What is in it | What it is worth |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Filings, results, broker notes, news, third-party series | Required, and read immediately. Everyone has it, so it rarely pays the excess return by itself |
| Paid | Bloomberg or Wind, expert networks, paid reports, chain databases | Standard for an institution. It saves time. It is not exclusive |
| Personal | Company IR, people in the chain, former colleagues, experts, distributors | This is the part that cannot be copied. It is also where the compliance line is sharpest: public facts and industry common sense only |

Four rules:

1. Hear the other side. Do not collect only the bull case.
2. Doubt every item until a second source agrees.
3. Record the source and the time. Do not pass on a rumor.
4. Keep a log, so a good note can be found later.

`[extension]` Minimum log fields: date, source layer, topic, conclusion, relation to the model (supports / conflicts / new), next action. Without the relation column, the log is a bookmark list.
