# 07 · Communications hardware

The rhythm is set by two lines together: carrier capex, and the generation upgrade.

## Drivers

1. The carrier capex cycle.
2. The generation upgrade: 2G, 3G, 4G, 5G, 6G.

## Segments

| Segment | Who | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RAN vendors | Huawei, Ericsson, Nokia, ZTE | The core of the industry, and the most concentrated |
| Optical | Modules, fiber, components | Lifted by AI compute |
| Base-station RF | Filters, power amplifiers, antennas | The most elastic in a 5G build |
| Switching and routing | Cisco, Arista | Data-center networking follows cloud capex |

## What to study

| Point | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Capex cycle | The major carriers publish the year's plan early. That plan is the compass |
| Build rhythm | Usually Q1 plans, Q2 tenders, Q3–Q4 delivery and revenue |
| Technology | 5G to 5.5G to 6G. Each generation lifts content per site |
| Export markets | Geopolitics matters. Sanctions on Huawei are the largest variable |

## Year clock

| When | Event | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January–March | Carriers publish the year's capex | Split 5G, compute, transport, and fixed. Note the year-on-year change |
| Q2 | Centralized tenders | Share won sets each company's revenue mix for the year |
| Q3–Q4 | Delivery and revenue | Compare tender share with revenue actually booked |
| All year | Sanctions and export controls | Size the effect on RAN vendors and on the domestic-substitution chain |

Optical sits on both carrier capex and cloud capex. In an AI cycle the second one weighs more. Do not read an optical module off the carrier budget alone (`05-ai-compute.md`).
