# 01 · What is different about tech hardware, and what an expectation gap is

## 1.1 Five traits, and what each forces you to do

| # | Trait | Meaning | Constraint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Strong cycles | Semiconductors and panels swing hard | Do not draw a straight line off the peak |
| 2 | Fast technology shifts | Under Moore's law a product life is short (an AI chip is 1–2 years) | The wrong route can be an exit |
| 3 | A global chain | Design in the US, manufacturing in Taiwan, materials in Japan | A political shock at one link moves the whole chain |
| 4 | Huge capex | A fab is tens of billions of dollars; a panel line is tens of billions of yuan | Scale and the cycle come with the assets |
| 5 | Concentrated customers | Apple, NVIDIA, and a few others take a large share of the chain | One customer's orders move the company's results |

`[extension]` Map each trait to an action:

| Trait | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Strong cycles | Place the cycle first (`02-general-framework.md`), then talk about valuation |
| Fast shifts | A route map: who backs each route, and the volume date |
| Global chain | Geography and the sanction or tariff exposure of each critical link |
| Huge capex | Depreciation as a share of cost, and the utilization level that breaks even |
| Concentrated customers | The large customer's capex or shipment guide is the input. Revenue is the output |

## 1.2 The point of the work is the expectation gap

Research finds where the consensus and the industry facts diverge, and checks that gap independently.

| Layer | Definition | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data gap | A different number for the same variable | Consensus is +10% units. The chain says +30% or −10% |
| Logic gap | A different cause, or a different winner | The market names company A. You find company B has the barrier and the faster share gain |
| Recognition gap | A different kind of event, or a different clock | The market calls a short bounce. You call a 3–5 year technology cycle |

`[extension]` Three questions before a conclusion:

1. What is the consensus, as a number or a story you can cite?
2. How far is your view, and is the gap data, logic, or recognition?
3. How many independent pieces of evidence? Fewer than 2 does not stand (`09-verification-methods.md`).

| Depth | Content | Bar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Know | What happened | A filing or a news item |
| Understand | Why | The chain, and supply versus demand |
| Anticipate | What happens next | This is the job |

The market prices the future, not the present. Once everyone knows the good news, it is already in the price.

`[extension]` Two working rules follow:

- When the good news lands (a confirmed large-customer order), that is often a point to reduce, unless it also lifts the future volume and price assumptions.
- The value of the work sits at the front of the signal chain: prototype, sample, qualification, design win, build plan. Confirmed revenue is late.
