# 03 · Semiconductors

The highest barriers and the strongest cycle in tech hardware. Study it **by link**. The eight links do not share a business model, a peer set, or a metric. Mixing them produces the wrong answer.

## Eight links

| Link | Model | Names | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EDA/IP | License plus royalty | Synopsys, Cadence, Arm | Customers, royalty rate, new products |
| Equipment | Heavy R&D | ASML, Applied Materials, NAURA | Orders, lead time, qualification |
| Materials | Many SKUs, slow qualification | Shin-Etsu, SUMCO, NSIG | Capacity, price, customer qualification |
| Fabless | Asset-light, high R&D | NVIDIA, Qualcomm, HiSilicon | Units, share, cadence |
| Foundry | Heavy assets, scale | TSMC, Samsung, SMIC | Utilization, node, capex |
| IDM | Design and manufacturing together | Intel, Samsung, Micron | Capacity, price, gross margin |
| OSAT | Labor and scale | ASE, JCET, TFME | Utilization, advanced packaging |
| Distribution | The spread, and an inventory cycle | WPG, Avnet, CECport | Inventory, receivables |

## The PE trap

Do not buy a semiconductor cycle name (memory, panels) on a high-looking cheapness at the peak: PE is lowest when earnings are best. Do not sell on a high PE at the trough: PE is highest, or meaningless, when earnings are worst. Buy when PE is highest. Sell when PE is lowest.

`[extension]`

- Peak: earnings are at a maximum, so P/E looks cheapest. It is the expensive point.
- Trough: earnings collapse or go negative, so P/E spikes or breaks. It is the cheap point.
- For memory, panels, mature-node foundry, and OSAT, anchor on PB, EV per unit of capacity, or the price-cycle position.

## Where to start, by link

| Link | Look first | Leading signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equipment | Orders on hand and lead time | Fab capex, 2–4 quarters ahead of equipment revenue |
| Materials | Customer qualification | A pass is the strongest signal before volume. The long cycle is both the moat and the risk |
| Design | Units and share | Tape-out, and a large customer's adoption |
| Foundry | Utilization and the node | Utilization under 70% is the loss warning (`02`) |
| IDM | Price and gross margin | NAND/DRAM spot, weekly (`10-tracking-system.md`) |
| OSAT | Utilization and advanced packaging | CoWoS-class capacity follows AI demand |
| Distribution | Inventory and receivables | Distributor inventory is one of the earliest signals in the industry |
| EDA/IP | Customers and the royalty rate | A new architecture or node, then the royalty ramp |
