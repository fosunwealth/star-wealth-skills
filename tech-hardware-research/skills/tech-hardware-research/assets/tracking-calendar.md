## By vertical, when that vertical is the subject

### Semiconductors

- Utilization, node progress, capex plan
- Distributor inventory and receivable aging (one of the earliest signals)

### Consumer electronics

- Apple's new model, started in Q3: direction, supplier list, content per unit, share, unit outlook
- Replacement cycle (about 36–48 months)
- Weeks of channel inventory

### AI compute

- Quarterly cloud capex guide and the AI share
- NVIDIA data-center revenue, then GPU units, then optical, PCB, and system demand. Three paths, checked against each other
- 800G units, 1.6T sample and volume dates, liquid-cooling penetration

### Displays

- Panel price by size (weekly or monthly), utilization, Korean shutdowns, China ramp

### Communications hardware

- The year's carrier capex plans (early in the year), then Q2 tender share, then Q3–Q4 delivery

### Automotive electronics

- Battery-electric, L2/L3, and lidar penetration
- Won programs and SOP dates. SiC annual price decline (often 10–20%)
